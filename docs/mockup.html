How color works
The group sets one brand color. The app derives three more by rotating its hue and fixing lightness, so every group gets a full palette without choosing one. Tints for tiles come from the same math.
Motion
A slow drifting glow in the header, a tick when the count changes, avatars that pop in after RSVP. All of it stops with reduced-motion settings.
Strength
Everything you liked in Clean, with more life. Derived colors keep a white-label group on-brand.
Watch for
Relative color syntax needs a current browser; older ones fall back to the single brand color. Derived hues can clash with an unusual brand color, so the group page should show a preview when branding is saved.

Confetti

Playful and colorful. Rounded tiles in four tints, a sticker date, and a burst when you RSVP.

What every look has to honor

A group sets one brand color (paid) and the app derives the rest. Free groups use the platform's default blue. Text on the brand color is chosen for contrast: marigold in the bar above gets dark text, not white. The readout shows the ratio.

All four looks use the same tokens and the same page structure. They differ in CSS and in a few partials (meeting header, "You're going" state, group header). Motion is decoration only and stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
